WHO WE ARE

One community, wherever we live.

ECIDF is a community-driven organization created to strengthen unity among Egbokor people at home and in the diaspora, preserve our heritage, and create a platform for meaningful participation in the community's future.

UnityIntegrityServiceCultural PrideAccountability

OUR VISION

A globally connected, culturally grounded and economically empowered Egbokor community.

OUR MISSION

  • Strengthen unity among Egbokor sons and daughters worldwide.
  • Promote cultural preservation and community identity.
  • Support development projects in Egbokor homeland.
  • Empower youth through education and mentorship.
  • Facilitate diaspora engagement and participation.
