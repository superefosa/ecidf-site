ECIDF exists to strengthen unity, preserve identity and create meaningful pathways for Egbokor's development.

WHO WE ARE

Rooted at home.
Connected worldwide.

Egbokor Community Integrated Development Foundation is a community-driven organization established to unite Egbokor people at home and abroad, promote cultural identity, support development initiatives and create a platform for collective participation.

VISION

To build a globally connected, culturally grounded and economically empowered Egbokor community.

MISSION

Unity. Cultural preservation. Community development. Youth empowerment. Meaningful global participation.
