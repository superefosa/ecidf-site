ABOUT ECIDF

Rooted in Egbokor.
Connected worldwide.

A community-driven foundation created to unite Egbokor indigenes, preserve our heritage and support sustainable development.

WHO WE ARE

One community. Shared responsibility.

ECIDF serves as a bridge between Egbokor people in the diaspora and our homeland, creating a structured platform for participation, collaboration and community development.

OUR VISION

A globally connected, culturally grounded and economically empowered Egbokor community.

OUR MISSION

Strengthen unity among Egbokor sons and daughters worldwide.
Promote cultural preservation and community identity.
Support development projects in Egbokor homeland.
Empower youth through education and mentorship.
Facilitate diaspora engagement and participation.
